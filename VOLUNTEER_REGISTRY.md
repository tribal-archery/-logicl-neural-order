## Activation requirements

A real person can be added only after identity, consent, role, contribution scope, and relevant evidence have been recorded and reviewed.

**UNASSIGNED** = vacancy only  
**IDENTIFIED** = real person identified  
**DOCUMENTED** = identity and role recorded  
**VERIFIED** = evidence checked  
**ACTIVE** = verified participant

## Public profile schema

**Name → public identity → role → scope → evidence references → verification date → status**

Do not publish unnecessary personal information.
