# Contact & Collaboration

## For organizations and research groups

Organizations, AI companies, research laboratories, engineers, and academic groups interested in the Neural Thinking Machine / WANGA architecture can contact the project through the public GitHub Issues channel.

### Technical collaboration

Use a new Issue with the title prefix:

`Collaboration: [organization/project]`

Please describe:

- organization or research group;
- technical area of interest;
- proposed collaboration;
- relevant public project or research reference;
- preferred next step.

### Research and evaluation

For benchmarking, AI reliability, drift detection, verification, provenance, orchestration, or model-index research, open an Issue with:

`Research: [topic]`

### Partnership inquiries

For organizations interested in discussing a technical or research partnership, open an Issue with:

`Partnership: [organization]`

Public GitHub Issues are intended only for initial contact and non-confidential discussion. Do not publish passwords, API keys, private credentials, personal phone numbers, private email addresses, confidential business information, or other sensitive material.

## Scope

This channel is for technical, scientific, research, and collaboration inquiries. It does not by itself constitute an offer to sell securities, tokens, financial products, or investment opportunities.

## Project status

The repository describes an evolving research architecture. Claims about AGI, production readiness, valuation, adoption, or independent validation require separate empirical evidence.
