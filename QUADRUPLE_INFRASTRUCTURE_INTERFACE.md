# Quadruple Multilevel Projection Project — Infrastructure Interface

## Purpose

This document defines the technical relationship between the Quadruple organization and the infrastructure built by Tribal Archery.

## Organization-side responsibilities

The Quadruple organization maintains the authoritative organizational layer for:

- financial organization;
- treasury records;
- issuance and capital structure;
- investor/stakeholder records;
- governance;
- organizational reporting.

## Infrastructure-side responsibilities

Tribal Archery builds and maintains supporting technical infrastructure for:

- identity and access;
- repositories and documentation;
- evidence and provenance;
- verification;
- workflow automation;
- technical research;
- auditability.

## Evidence boundary

Technical infrastructure may preserve and verify records, but preservation does not itself establish the legal truth of an organizational or financial claim.

For each material claim:

**CLAIM → PRIMARY SOURCE → PROVENANCE → VERIFICATION → PUBLIC STATUS**

## Personnel interface

The Quadruple organization can supply real personnel records and role evidence. Tribal Archery can provide the technical registry and verification structure.

No placeholder profile is presented as a real volunteer or employee.
