# Standards Crosswalk

## Purpose
Maintain a living map between the project's internal architecture and established public AI governance frameworks.

## Initial reference
NIST AI RMF 1.0 provides four functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE. The project's internal governance layer can be mapped against these functions for research and gap analysis.

## Important distinction
A crosswalk is an analytical comparison. It does not imply endorsement, certification, compliance, accreditation, or official affiliation.

## Planned mappings
- Governance policies → internal policy layer
- Risk/context analysis → project and evidence context
- Measurement/evaluation → AI assurance and drift forensics
- Risk treatment → decision gates and remediation
- Documentation/provenance → audit layer
- Human oversight → roles and escalation

Future versions may add other public standards and frameworks after source verification.
