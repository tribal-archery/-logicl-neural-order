# AI Assurance and Drift Forensics

## Objective
Connect AI evaluation and drift-forensics evidence to governance decisions.

## Evidence chain
Baseline → Observation → Difference → Classification → Propagation → Persistence → Recovery → Verification → Audit

## Drift governance
A detected change is first treated as an observation requiring investigation. Attribution, causality, severity, and impact are separate analytical questions.

## Decision gate
A governance action should require:
- defined evidence requirements;
- reproducible comparison;
- documented uncertainty;
- identified affected system/version;
- verification or independent review where appropriate;
- an audit record of the decision.

## Relationship to NTM/WANGA
The Neural Thinking Machine can route evidence, verification, and specialist analysis while preserving task identity, provenance, and audit information.
