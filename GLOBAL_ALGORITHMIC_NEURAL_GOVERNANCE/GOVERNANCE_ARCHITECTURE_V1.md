# Governance Architecture V1

## Governance layers
1. Mission and scope
2. Policies and controls
3. Evidence and provenance
4. Measurement and evaluation
5. Verification and validation
6. Human review and escalation
7. Decision gates
8. Audit and continuous improvement

## Technical interface
Governance decisions should be represented as explicit policies, constraints, evidence requirements, decision gates, and audit events that can connect to the Neural Thinking Machine / WANGA orchestration layer.

## Separation of functions
Generation, verification, governance review, and final decision recording are separate functions. A model that generates an output does not automatically become the verifier or final authority for that output.

## Boundary condition
The system provides governance mechanisms for participating projects and organizations. It does not assume authority over external systems without a separately established legal, contractual, or institutional basis.
