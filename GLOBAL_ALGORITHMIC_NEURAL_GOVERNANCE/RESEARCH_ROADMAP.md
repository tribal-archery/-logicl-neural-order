# Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Define governance vocabulary.
- Establish evidence and provenance requirements.
- Formalize decision gates.
- Connect governance events to the NTM audit model.

## Phase 2 — Assurance
- Define reproducible AI evaluation protocols.
- Integrate drift-forensics classifications.
- Build verification workflows.
- Create governance audit records.

## Phase 3 — Interoperability
- Expand standards crosswalks.
- Define machine-readable governance policies.
- Test interfaces between governance and orchestration.

## Phase 4 — Research Network
- Publish technical findings.
- Establish scientific collaboration protocols.
- Develop research funding and contributor accounting models.

## Success criterion
Progress is measured by reproducibility, documented evidence, working interfaces, and independently reviewable results—not by institutional claims or nominal scale.
