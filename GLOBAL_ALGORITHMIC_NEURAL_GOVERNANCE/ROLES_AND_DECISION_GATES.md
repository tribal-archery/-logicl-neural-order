# Roles and Decision Gates

## Roles
- Researcher — formulates and tests hypotheses.
- Evaluator — measures system behavior against defined criteria.
- Forensic Analyst — investigates drift and evidence chains.
- Verifier — independently checks specified claims or outputs.
- Governance Reviewer — evaluates policy, risk, and escalation requirements.
- Decision Owner — records the authorized project decision.
- Auditor — reviews provenance, controls, and historical records.

## Rule
No role should be inferred merely from a model's ability to produce text. Role assignment is an explicit system property.

## Decision gates
Evidence Gate → Verification Gate → Governance Gate → Decision Record

Each gate should have a documented input, criterion, responsible role, outcome, and provenance reference.
