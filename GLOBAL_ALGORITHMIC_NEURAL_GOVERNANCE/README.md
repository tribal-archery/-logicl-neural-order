# Global Algorithmic Neural Governance

## Purpose
A research and engineering program for AI governance, assurance, evaluation, drift forensics, algorithmic accountability, and interfaces between governance policy and executable AI systems.

## Status and boundary
This is a research and standards program. It does not claim governmental, diplomatic, sovereign, banking, or regulatory authority over external organizations or states.

## Core areas
- AI Governance
- AI Assurance and Evaluation
- AI Drift Forensics
- Evidence and Provenance
- Human Oversight and Decision Gates
- Standards Crosswalks
- Algorithmic Accountability
- Research Funding Architecture

## Architectural relationship
The program sits at the organization level and connects to Neural Thinking Machine / WANGA, Neural OS, AI Drift Forensics, AI²³¹ Research, and Ontometric Research.

## Reference orientation
The architecture is designed to be compared with established public AI governance and risk-management frameworks. NIST AI RMF is one reference point; its GOVERN function emphasizes policies, accountability, documentation, and continuous risk management across the AI lifecycle.

This repository's framework remains an independent research architecture and should not be presented as an official implementation of NIST or any other external institution.
